## 🎬 Detailed Scene-by-Scene Script

### 🔴 SCENE 1: THE HOOK (0:00 - 0:30) — Emotional Family Care
* **Visual**: Camera pans into a dimly lit modern living room with a large 4K TV showing HomeIQ.
* **On Screen**: High-visibility Crimson Hazard Overlay slides smoothly from the top of the Fire TV screen:
  > `⚠ Family Guardian Alert: Kitchen Stove ON for 3 Hours`  
  > `Elder Care Sensor: No kitchen motion detected for 180 mins. Auto-shutoff in 60s.`
* **Voiceover (Warm, Empathetic Tone)**:
  > *"It’s 2:14 AM. In Bengaluru, elderly grandmother Savithri lives with her family. Three hours ago, she brewed warm milk on the kitchen induction stove and forgot to turn it off before falling asleep in her bedroom.*  
  > *In most smart homes, this is an undetected catastrophe waiting to happen.*  
  > *In HomeIQ, it’s an automatic resolution."*
* **On Screen Action**: Fire TV cursor selects `[🛑 Auto-Shutoff Now]`. The stove instantly cuts power, wattage drops to 0 W, and HUD pops:
  > `🛑 Family Guardian: Kitchen Stove powered OFF automatically. Hazard averted!`
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"HomeIQ doesn't just monitor your home. It protects the people you love."*

---

### 🔵 SCENE 2: ACT 1 (0:30 - 1:15) — The Command Center on Fire TV
* **Visual**: Main Dashboard transitions into view (`Screen 1: MAIN DASHBOARD`).
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"HomeIQ transforms your Fire TV into an ambient, 10-foot smart home command center built natively with Jetpack Compose for TV.*  
  > *At a glance, see your 24-hour load curve, daily energy goal progress ring, real-time microclimate weather, and active security status."*
* **On Screen Action**:
  - D-Pad moves smoothly between room tabs: `Living Room` ➔ `Kitchen` ➔ `Master Bedroom` ➔ `Garage`.
  - Stepper controls (`+` / `—`) adjust AC temperature from 24°C to 26°C and dim ceiling lights to 80%.
  - User clicks `3. ENERGY ANALYTICS`:
    - Reactive Bézier Spline Wave curve renders smoothly.
    - User taps through `Today` ➔ `Week` ➔ `Month` ➔ `Year`.
    - Dynamic peak callout badge tracks curve apex (`6 PM • 4.8 kW`, `Wk 2 • 38 kWh`, `Jul • 142 kWh`).
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"13 devices across 4 rooms, real-time cubic Bézier spline telemetry, and instant 1-click controls — all engineered for effortless remote navigation from your couch."*

---

### 🟢 SCENE 3: ACT 2 (1:15 - 2:00) — Meet "IQ": The AI Coach (Amazon Bedrock)
* **Visual**: Switch to Tab 5 (`5. AI ASSISTANT / VOICE COMMAND`).
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Meet IQ — not a generic robotic chatbot, but an emotionally intelligent smart home companion powered by Amazon Bedrock running Anthropic Claude 3 Haiku.*  
  > *IQ understands your family by name and proactively finds ways to save money without sacrificing comfort."*
* **On Screen Action**:
  - User taps quick chip: `"How to save ₹500 on bill?"`
  - Bedrock processes real-time home telemetry and responds:
    > *"Hey Alex! I noticed your living room AC ran 4 hours at 20°C. Switching to Eco Saver 26°C will save ₹85 this month while keeping the room cool."*
  - User clicks dynamic toggle: `💡 Turn OFF Lights`.
    - Instant two-way synchronization: all 13 devices in the house turn off! Button dynamically flips to `💡 Turn ON Lights`.
  - User types custom query in the prompt box: `"Optimize solar battery schedule"`.
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"IQ bridges hardware telemetry with generative AI to make energy savings feel effortless and personal."*

---

### 🟡 SCENE 4: ACT 3 (2:00 - 2:30) — Family Guardian & Safety Vault
* **Visual**: User opens `🛡️ Family Guardian & Safety Vault`.
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Beyond energy, HomeIQ brings unparalleled peace of mind with the Family Guardian safety vault.*  
  > *Elder Care Mode continuously monitors routine anomalies — tracking ambient room temperature, motion gaps, and night wandering.*  
  > *Child Safety alerts notify parents if perimeter locks disarm during school hours.*  
  > *Meanwhile, hardware tripwires monitor 40kA surge arresters, zero-ppm gas sensors, and an emergency 10-millisecond main breaker trip."*
* **On Screen Action**:
  - Showcase 4 family members: Alex (Admin), Grandma Savithri (Monitored), Priya (Office), Aarav (School).
  - Tap `🏆 Eco Challenges`: Show 7-Day Eco Streak 🔥, Family Leaderboard (Alex vs Grandma Savithri), and unlocked badges (`First Week Champion`, `Solar Star`).
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Daily challenges turn energy conservation into a friendly family game."*

### 🟣 SCENE 5: CLOSING (2:30 - 3:00) — Cross-Device Voice & Vision

* **On Screen Action**: Living Room AC shifts to 26°C with green Eco Saver badge!
* **Voiceover**:
